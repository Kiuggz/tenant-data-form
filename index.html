Prospective Tenant Application Form

Please complete this form and ensure that all required information is provided accurately and in full. The information you provide will be treated confidentially and used solely for tenant screening, tenancy processing, preparation of the tenancy agreement, and verification purposes. By submitting this form, you consent to the collection and use of your information by our office and the respective landlord for purposes related to your tenancy application.

1 Property Details

Use the name shown by the caretaker, agent or landlord.
Enter the unit, room, house or door number being applied for.

2 Personal Information

Write your full legal name as it appears on your ID or Passport.
Use the same document number for the copy you will send after submission.

3 Employment & Income

State your employer/business and the work you do.
Give a supervisor, HR contact, colleague or business contact who can confirm your work.
This should be the referee's own phone number, not yours.

4 Next of Kin Details

Complete this only if your marital status is married.
If you share one number, the form will warn you but still allow it.
Provide a close relative or trusted contact who is not your spouse.
Use the next of kin's own reachable number.

5 Your Current Home

Give the estate/area and town where you currently live.
If you deal with a caretaker or agent, give the person who can confirm your tenancy.
This should be the landlord/caretaker/agent contact, not your own number.
Optional; fill this if rent or communication goes through an agent/caretaker.
Optional; paste a payment reference or brief details if available.

6 Vetting Questions

Give a brief factual reason, for example work, space, rent, family or location.
Include yourself and anyone else who will regularly live in the premises.
Fill this only if someone else will pay rent on your behalf.
Use the payer's own phone number.
Give the expected period; this is for planning and agreement preparation.
Optional; note practical requests before occupation, if any.
This helps confirm care and safety where pets are allowed.
Optional; add anything you want clarified before the tenancy is confirmed.

7 Declaration

I hereby declare that the information provided above is true and complete to the best of my knowledge. I understand that providing false or misleading information may result in the rejection of my tenancy application.
